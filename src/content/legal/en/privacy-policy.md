## 2. Scope

This Policy explains how Ladyfit processes personal data when a user:

- visits the website;
- adds products to the cart;
- uses search and filters;
- provides name, phone and email;
- chooses to remember buyer details;
- starts a purchase through WhatsApp;
- contacts Ladyfit regarding an order;
- uses features involving analytics cookies, where consent has been given.

## 4. Purposes and legal grounds

### Preparing and performing a purchase
Name, phone, email and order data are processed to prepare purchase intentions, communicate via WhatsApp, confirm availability, manage orders and support payment/delivery arrangements.

**Legal basis:** steps requested prior to entering into a contract and performance of the contractual relationship.

### Remembering details for future purchases
Where the user selects the option to remember details, Ladyfit may maintain the technical association required to pre-fill name, phone and email for up to **365 days**.

**Legal basis:** consent.

Consent may be withdrawn at any time through preference controls or by contacting Ladyfit. Withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before withdrawal.

### Persistent cart
The cart may remain available for up to **30 days** so the user can resume product selection. The cart does not reserve stock.

### Security
Technical data may be processed where strictly necessary to protect the website, investigate errors, prevent abuse and maintain system integrity.

The legal basis may include legitimate interests in the secure and reliable operation of the service, subject to the rights of the individual.

### Legal obligations
Data may be retained or disclosed where required by an applicable legal obligation.

### Google Analytics
Non-essential analytics technologies are activated only after consent.

**Legal basis:** consent.

## 5. Required and optional data

Name, phone and email are required to start the WhatsApp purchase flow.

The option to **remember details for future purchases** is separate from the purchase and must remain optional.

## 6. Retention

### Cart
Up to 30 days after the last update unless deleted earlier.

### Remembered buyer details
Up to 365 days where the user has enabled the feature, unless consent is withdrawn or the details are deleted earlier.

### Orders and communications
For as long as reasonably necessary to manage the customer relationship, resolve purchase-related matters, meet applicable obligations and establish or defend rights where relevant.

### Analytics
According to the retention settings adopted in the analytics service while consent remains valid.
