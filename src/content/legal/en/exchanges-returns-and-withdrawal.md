# Exchanges, Returns and Right of Withdrawal — Ladyfit

## 1. Different situations

Ladyfit distinguishes between:

1. statutory withdrawal from a distance purchase;
2. voluntary commercial exchanges;
3. defective, non-conforming or incorrect goods.

Statutory consumer rights are not replaced by voluntary commercial policies.

## 2. Right of withdrawal

Where legally applicable, a consumer has **14 days** to withdraw from a distance contract without giving a reason.

For goods, the period normally starts when the consumer, or a third party other than the carrier indicated by the consumer, takes physical possession.

## 4. Returning the product

After notice of withdrawal, the consumer must return the product without undue delay and within the applicable legal period.

Proof of return should be retained.

## 5. Handling

The consumer may handle the product only to the extent necessary to establish its nature, characteristics, size, fit and normal functioning.

The consumer may be responsible for diminished value resulting from handling beyond what is necessary.

## 6. Return costs

For voluntary withdrawal, direct return costs are borne by the consumer unless Ladyfit agreed otherwise or the law requires a different outcome.

Different rules apply to returns caused by lack of conformity.

## 7. Refund

Where withdrawal is validly exercised, the refund includes legally refundable amounts and, where applicable, the cost of the least expensive standard delivery method originally offered.

The refund is made within the applicable legal period. Where legally permitted, Ladyfit may wait until the goods or evidence of return are received.

## 8. Refund method

Refunds use the original payment method unless a different method is expressly agreed without additional cost to the consumer.

## 9. Exceptions

Withdrawal rights may be excluded in legally defined cases.

Potential examples, only when applicable, include:

- goods made to consumer specifications or clearly personalised;
- sealed goods unsuitable for return for health or hygiene reasons once unsealed.

Exceptions should only be relied upon where the relevant legal conditions are met.

## 10. Size or colour exchanges

Outside a specific statutory right, size or colour exchanges may be offered as a commercial policy subject to stock availability, product condition, communicated conditions, price differences and agreed transport costs.

## 11. Defects and lack of conformity

Where a product lacks conformity, the consumer should contact Ladyfit.

Ladyfit may request order number, product details, photographs and a description of the issue.

Depending on applicable law, remedies may include repair, replacement, price reduction or termination.

## 12. Statutory conformity period

For new movable goods sold to consumers in Portugal, statutory seller liability for lack of conformity may apply for **three years from delivery**, subject to applicable conditions.

Specific statutory remedies may apply where lack of conformity appears within the first 30 days after delivery.

## 13. Incorrect item

If the delivered product does not match the confirmed order, the customer should contact Ladyfit. This should not be treated as a voluntary withdrawal attributable to the customer.
