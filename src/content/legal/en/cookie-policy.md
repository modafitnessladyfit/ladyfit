# Cookie Policy — Ladyfit

## 1. Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files or identifiers stored or accessed on a user's device.

The website may also use technically similar mechanisms to maintain functionality and preferences.

## 2. Categories

### Strictly necessary
Used for features requested by the user and essential website operation, including cart identification, administrative session and security.

Cart persistence may last up to **30 days**.

### Remember my details
Where the user voluntarily chooses to save details for future purchases, an identifier may be used to retrieve name, phone and email.

Maximum planned duration: **365 days**.

This feature should only be enabled after the user's choice.

### Analytics
Ladyfit may use **Google Analytics** to understand website use in aggregate.

Non-essential analytics technologies must remain disabled until consent is given.

## 3. Consent

On the first visit, where non-essential cookies are present, the user must be able to:

- accept analytics;
- reject analytics;
- manage preferences.

Rejecting analytics must not prevent basic shop, catalogue or cart functionality.

## 4. Withdrawal

Users must be able to change their choice later through **Manage Cookies**.

Withdrawing consent must be as easy as giving it.

## 5. Mandatory banner copy

### Title
**Your privacy matters**

### Text
**We use essential cookies to operate Ladyfit and the shopping cart. With your permission, we also use Google Analytics cookies to understand how the website is used and improve the experience. You can accept, reject or manage your preferences.**

### Buttons
- **Accept analytics**
- **Reject analytics**
- **Manage preferences**

Analytics options must not be preselected.

## 6. Preference panel

### Essential
**Always active**  
Required for website operation, security and the cart.

### Remember my details
**Optional**  
Allows the website to remember the preference to retrieve name, phone and email for future purchases for up to 365 days.

### Analytics
**Optional**  
Enables Google Analytics to measure website usage and performance.

## 7. Third-party services

Where analytics consent has been given, Google Analytics may use its own technologies subject to the provider's terms and policies.

The presence of WhatsApp or Instagram buttons does not itself authorise the prior placement of non-essential cookies from those services.

## 8. Browser controls

Users can also delete or block cookies through browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may affect functionality such as cart persistence.
