# Shipping, Delivery and Collection — Ladyfit

## 1. Operating model

The website does not automatically calculate or close transport arrangements.

After the customer starts the purchase on WhatsApp, Ladyfit agrees directly:

- shipping/delivery method;
- destination;
- cost;
- estimated time;
- collection where applicable;
- necessary instructions.

## 2. Information before confirmation

Before final order confirmation, the customer should be informed, where applicable, about shipping cost, delivery method, time/estimate, collection conditions and geographical limitations.

## 3. Timing

The timing communicated during customer service is an estimate or commitment according to what is expressly agreed.

Ladyfit should inform the customer of any known material delay.

## 4. Address

Where delivery to an address is used, the customer is responsible for providing complete and accurate information.

If delivery fails due to an incorrect or incomplete address supplied by the customer, new arrangements should be agreed directly.

## 5. Receipt

Customers should check the external condition of the package and promptly report obvious delivery-related issues.

This does not remove statutory conformity rights that may be exercised later.

## 6. Costs

Any shipping or delivery charges are communicated through WhatsApp before confirmation.

No additional charge should be imposed without the customer's knowledge and acceptance.

## 7. Collection

Where collection is available, Ladyfit and the customer should agree the location, time, order identification and necessary conditions.

## 8. Deliveries outside Portugal

Where delivery to another European country is available, Ladyfit confirms availability, price, method, estimate and any limitations in advance.
